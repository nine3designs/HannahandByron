2016 · The Introduction

A birthday party, a bad playlist, and a good conversation

We met at a mutual friend's birthday dinner — Byron arrived late, Hannah had already claimed the seat next to the good radiator. Two hours and one terrible playlist later, neither of us wanted to leave. We exchanged numbers under the pretence of "sharing photos from the night." Neither of us ever sent a single photo.

A flat with a broken boiler and the best two years of our lives

We moved in together far too early, into a flat with a boiler that worked maybe half the time. We adopted a very opinionated cat, learned to cook properly, and figured out how to argue about the important things and let go of the rest.
