Timeline

The day, hour by hour

A rough guide so you know where to be and when. We'll keep this updated as details are confirmed.

12:30

Guests arrive

Please make your way to The Glass House and be seated by 12:45 — the ceremony room doors close promptly at 1:00pm.

1:00

Ceremony

We say "I do" in the glasshouse itself, surrounded by the people who matter most. Expect it to run around 30 minutes.

1:45

Drinks & canapés

Out to the garden for drinks, canapés, and confetti photos while we sneak off for a few portraits.

4:00

Wedding breakfast

Speeches, a sit-down meal, and probably a few happy tears. Seating plan will be on display as you enter.

7:00

Evening guests arrive

Welcome to anyone joining us just for the party — grab a drink and find us on the dance floor.

7:30

First dance & dancing

The band starts, the first dance happens (badly, but with enthusiasm), and the evening properly begins.

12:00

Carriages

The night wraps up at midnight. Taxis and details on getting home are on the travel page.
