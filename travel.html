Getting to us

The Glass House, Staining — full address and directions below.

✈

By car

The venue sits just off the A583, around 20 minutes from Blackpool and 40 minutes from Preston. Free on-site parking is available for all guests for the full day and overnight.

Open in Google Maps
🚆

By train

The nearest station is Blackpool North, roughly 15 minutes away by taxi. We're arranging a shuttle for the evening — details to follow closer to the day.

🏨

Staying over

For those travelling to celebrate with us, there are a couple of nearby hotel options we’d recommend. Both are just a short drive from The Glass House. Village Hotel Blackpool A great option for anyone looking for a comfortable hotel with a bar, restaurant and leisure facilities. It’s around a 5-minute drive from the venue. Premier Inn Blackpool East A convenient and more budget-friendly option, around a 5–7 minute drive from the venue. We’ll be staying in the Staining Luxury Lodges at the venue, so we’ll be nice and close by! 🥂
