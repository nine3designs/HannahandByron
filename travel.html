Travel

Getting Here

Everything you need to know about travelling to The Glass House and where to stay for the weekend.

🚗

By Car & Taxi

The Glass House is located at Staining Lodge, just minutes from Blackpool and easily accessible from the M55.


Complimentary parking is available onsite for all guests throughout the day. Should you wish to leave your vehicle overnight, we kindly ask that it is parked in the main car park.

The Glass House car park is situated closest to the venue entrance for convenient access during the day.

Open in Google Maps
🚆

By Train

The nearest station is Poulton-le-Fylde, approximately 10 minutes by taxi from the venue.


Blackpool North station is around 15 minutes away and offers direct rail connections from many major UK cities.


Taxis are usually available at both stations, and Uber operates throughout the area.

Getting Home
